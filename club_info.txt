AWS Student Builders Group

The AWS Student Builders Group carries out general AWS student-club activities.
AWS has four teams: Events, Operations, Tech, and PR.
First-year students cannot apply to AWS.
AWS applications open every semester.
AWS recruitment happens once per semester.
AWS Tech team selection involves resume shortlisting followed by an interview.
Other AWS teams select members through an application form and questions.
The AWS Tech team helps organize workshops, tech talks, and other AWS initiatives.
AWS members must maintain 75% attendance in technical sessions.
The AWS Tech team meets every Saturday.
The AWS lead is KR.
Students can belong to both AWS and GDG Campus.
AWS inauguration is on 29th and 30th September 2026.
Campus:
- JSSATEB

GDG Campus

GDG Campus organizes events.
GDG has three teams: Tech, PR, and Events.
Students from all years can apply to GDG Campus.
GDG recruitment happens once per year.
GDG recruitment starts at the beginning of odd semesters.
GDG has interview rounds for all teams.
GDG has no AI team.
GDG members must maintain 70% attendance.
Members who do not meet the attendance requirement will not be taken into the club the next year.
GDG conducts three workshops each semester.
GDG workshops can cover cybersecurity and other topics.
Students can suggest workshop topics.
Students can belong to both GDG Campus and AWS.
The GDG lead is M.
There is no specific room for GDG.
